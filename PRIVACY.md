This notice explains how EQphile Studio handles information. External websites
and services have their own privacy policies. The support mailbox uses
Microsoft Exchange Online.

Free distribution
All features bundled with this version are free to use. The app contains
no paid-tier checkout, subscription system, license activation, payment SDK
or device-license tracking. A donation is not required to use any feature.

Audio processing
Local files and captured system audio are processed on your Mac. The app
does not send audio samples to the developer, create recordings of captured
system audio, or include an advertising or usage-analytics client. Capturing
system audio requires macOS permission and is controlled in the application.
You can revoke permission in macOS System Settings.

Local information
The app stores its preferences, library references, source-file paths and
presets on your Mac, in its own preferences and Application Support location.
Track metadata and artwork are used to display playback and choose background
colors. These local files are not sent to the developer automatically.
Exports you request create files at the destination you choose. To remove
local application data, quit the app and remove its EQphile Studio data and
preferences; this does not delete your original music files. Deleting only
the app bundle may leave those settings behind.

Optional EQphile Notch
When enabled in Settings, the floating player uses pointer position and mouse
events locally to expand, contract and pass through clicks outside its controls.
Those events are not stored or transmitted. The setting is saved on your Mac;
disabling it removes the floating panel and its mouse-event monitors.

Spotify
With your macOS Automation permission, the app communicates with the local
Spotify app to read playback metadata and send the playback commands you
request. Artwork is downloaded from approved Spotify image hosts over HTTPS.
Those servers necessarily receive a network request, including the connection's
IP address and the requested image address. The application does not request
your Spotify password or embed a Spotify account token.
Artwork requests use a dedicated temporary network session without disk HTTP
caching, cookies or stored credentials. Only the current decoded cover is held
in memory and is released on disconnect. Redirects are limited to the same
approved image-host families. EQphile Studio may clear its disposable response
cache and cookies for those image hosts. Your music, presets, library and
settings are not removed.

Apple Music / Music for Mac
With your macOS Automation permission, EQphile Studio reads the current track,
artist, album, duration, playback state and artwork from the local Music app.
It sends only the playback, seek, shuffle and repeat commands you request.
This integration does not request an Apple account password, developer token
or access to your account, and does not upload your library to the developer.
Playing a service subscription remains the responsibility of the Music app.

When Music does not expose a cover through scripting, the app may use matching
artwork published locally by macOS. It makes no catalogue search or Apple
image-server request for this integration. If neither local source supplies a
matching image, the cover remains unavailable. Decoded covers are cached in
memory with a size limit. Images from a different process or unmatched track
are rejected. Music and macOS may independently use their own network services.

Browser playback
The browser integration reads media information and artwork published locally
by macOS and sends supported playback commands to the selected source. It
does not inspect browser history, cookies, or the contents of web pages.
This integration uses a locally bundled adapter; the app does not send this
media information to the developer.

Updates
This version does not check an online release channel or install updates
automatically. The Updates window links to the developer's website, which opens
only when you choose it. That website receives the request and related
connection information under its own privacy policy. Checking for updates
does not upload your music library or diagnostic logs.

External links and contributions
Website, GitHub, PayPal and Ko-fi links open an external service only when
you choose them. No contribution is required or checked to enable app features.
Those services receive your connection information and process any information
you enter there under their own policies. The app does not handle payment
credentials, receive your card or PayPal login details, initiate a payment,
embed a payment SDK, or receive payment-status callbacks.

Although the app does not receive payment credentials, the developer as payee
can receive supporter information from Ko-fi or the payment provider: for
example display/contact details, contribution amount/date and an optional
message. Ko-fi shares the supporter's display name and email with the creator;
a public/private message setting is controlled on that external service.
Payment providers may share additional transaction details under their terms.
This information can be used to identify a contribution, answer a related
request, handle a payment dispute and meet applicable recordkeeping duties.
Contributions do not create an app account or a paid license. No marketing
mailing list or in-app supporter profiling is operated by the app.

The developer uses the minimum supporter information necessary for these
purposes, on the basis of legitimate interests in administering voluntary
contributions and answering related requests, and legal obligations where
applicable recordkeeping duties require it. Records are retained as necessary
for these purposes and relevant obligations. The external services' privacy
policies describe their processing and international-transfer arrangements;
links are provided below. Contact the developer with questions about the
supporter information they receive.

Support and personal information

correspondence at eqphile@maurotarazona.com. Support messages are used to answer
questions, investigate reported issues and communicate about those requests.
Messages can contain your email address, name, message and attachments you
choose to send. Sending a message is optional; audio processing does not
require contacting support. The developer relies on legitimate interests in
answering voluntary enquiries, investigating reports and following up support.
This does not authorize unrelated advertising or excessive collection.

Report a Problem, feature suggestions and the email link in About open your

and send the message yourself. The app has no automatic feedback or
diagnostic-upload mechanism. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or
private music files; do not put private information into public issue reports.
