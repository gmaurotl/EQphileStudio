# Changelog

## 0.17.8 beta (build 40)

First public beta of EQphile Studio for Apple Silicon Macs.

- Local playback, 20-band and parametric EQ, tone controls, and Mac Audio.
- Playback information and supported controls for Spotify, Music and supported browsers.
- Spotify actions are enabled when their availability can be verified. The
  progress bar remains readable when seeking is unavailable.
- Optional EQphile Notch, light and dark themes, and four interface languages.

See [Installation](INSTALLATION.md) and [Compatibility](COMPATIBILITY.md).
