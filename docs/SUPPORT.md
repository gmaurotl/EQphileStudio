# Support

Support and privacy contact: **eqphile@maurotarazona.com**.

Developer website: [maurotarazona.com](https://maurotarazona.com).
The app's Help menu opens your mail composer; you decide what to send.

## Reporting a problem

Include the EQphile Studio version and build shown in About, macOS version,
Mac chip, output device, selected source, reproduction steps and expected result.
For playback issues, say whether
they affect local files, Spotify, Music / Apple Music or browser / YouTube.
Mention whether Mac Audio or EQphile Notch is enabled.

Send only what is needed. Do not attach passwords, payment credentials, private
music or full personal file paths. Public issues and their attachments are visible to anyone. Use email for private material.

## Music and artwork

For Music / Apple Music, start a track in Music, select that source in Studio
and grant Music Automation permission if requested. A missing cover does not
necessarily indicate a playback failure: Studio uses only artwork supplied
locally by Music or matching macOS media information. It does not search Apple's
catalog for a replacement. Describe whether Music itself shows a cover and
whether Studio still shows the correct title and artist.

Selecting a playback source and enabling Mac Audio are separate actions.
For an audio-processing report, include whether Mac Audio is enabled and which
output device and format are selected. Do not send music samples unless a
separate request and suitable rights justify it.

## Support mail and privacy

New support correspondence uses a Microsoft Exchange Online shared mailbox.
The developer reads and replies using Outlook or Outlook on the web. See
[Privacy](PRIVACY.md) for the current service and historical correspondence.

Ordinary support mail has no fixed deletion date or automatic deletion rule.
It is retained as necessary for support, follow-up, security or claims, with
individual assessment of necessity and erasure requests. Applicable legal
obligations may require minimal records to be retained separately.

Contributions do not purchase a response-time promise or preferential support.
Security concerns should be reported privately; see [Security](SECURITY.md).
