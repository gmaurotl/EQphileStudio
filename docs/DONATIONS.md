# Optional tips

EQphile Studio and all features included in its distributed version are free.
You can use the app without contributing. A contribution does not unlock any
feature, activate or extend a license, buy priority support, or guarantee future work.

If you choose to support the developer:

- [Invite Mauro to a coffee on Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/gmaurotl)
- [Contribute through PayPal](https://paypal.me/MauroTarazonaLevano)

These are external services. You choose the amount and review the currency,
recipient, fees and any recurring option there before authorizing a payment.
The app itself does not start payments or subscriptions and receives no
payment-status callbacks. No payment or donation is required to keep using it.

Ko-fi/payment providers share some supporter details with the developer,
including a display/contact identity and transaction information. Public or
private messages follow the choices and policies of that service. Read
[Privacy](PRIVACY.md), [Ko-fi privacy](https://more.ko-fi.com/privacy) and
[PayPal privacy](https://www.paypal.com/es/legalhub/paypal/privacy-full).

For mistakes or questions, contact eqphile@maurotarazona.com. Provider rules and
mandatory rights apply. This page makes no charitable-status or tax-deduction claim.
